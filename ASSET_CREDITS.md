# Asset ownership and credits

## Book cover and hero animation

The cover is client-supplied publisher artwork. The animation uses client-supplied video frames stored as local WebP atlases and a reduced-motion poster. These are not public-domain assets. The owner confirmed permission and the animation licence on 13 October 2026; The Web Guys should retain supporting licence records privately. No original MOV, transfer ZIP or publisher manuscript is included.

The cover master is retained intact. Smaller WebP variants and a social JPEG are generated at build time. The butterfly animation is decorative, runs briefly, and settles on the cover. It does not require an external media service.

## Fee butterflies and favicon

The current fee symbols and Jeanie Calder monogram are original vector graphics created for this website. The older third-party butterfly photograph is no longer used or included in the current source/output. Historical Git revisions retain their original attribution records.

## Third-party software and fonts

Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are supplied by Fontsource and retain their SIL Open Font Licences. React, Radix UI, Lucide and other packages retain their own licences. The source dependency lockfile identifies the exact versions. The static handover package includes third-party licence files alongside the deployment files; do not strip required notices.

No blanket licence is granted over the website copy, book artwork or supplied animation by these notes. Confirm the agency/client contractual ownership arrangements separately.
